# Google Play store listing

Copy these into Play Console > Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing.

## App name (30 characters max)

Roundsman Trash Tycoon

(Several other games on Google Play and the App Store already use the name "Trash Tycoon". Putting your Roundsman brand in front keeps the listing distinct, which helps with Google's rule against names that could be confused with another app. The name on the phone's home screen stays "Trash Tycoon".)

## Short description (80 characters max)

Grow a trash company from one pickup truck to the biggest hauler in the state.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Start with one pickup truck, a trailer and 400 homes in Maple Hollow that need their cans emptied. Where you take it from there is up to you.

BUILD YOUR FLEET
Buy rear loaders, automated side loaders, front loaders for business dumpsters and roll-off trucks for construction sites. Every truck needs a driver and a place to park, so hire your crew and open new yards as you grow.

GROW YOUR WAY
Win the franchise in new towns, start a recycling program and watch material prices move every day, build a sorting plant, open a transfer station and finally your own landfill, where other haulers pay you to tip. Run ads, sign contracts with HOAs, strip malls and builders, and upgrade your office software one module at a time.

KEEP YOUR CUSTOMERS HAPPY
Sign up homes faster than your trucks can serve them and pickups get missed. Your reputation drops, sign-ups slow and customers leave. Breakdowns happen too: hire mechanics, or pay to rush a roadcall and get the truck back on its route.

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO EARN
83 trophies to collect, from your first route to a million cans at the curb. Every trophy adds to your revenue for good, and the next three goals are always on screen.

SELL AND START AGAIN
Once your company has earned a million dollars, sell it for Legacy stars and start over with one pickup, stronger every time. The towns never run out.

PLAYS ANYWHERE
Your crews keep running routes while you are away. The game works with no signal, asks for no permissions and has no ads, accounts or tracking.

Built on the Roundsman route software for residential waste collection.
