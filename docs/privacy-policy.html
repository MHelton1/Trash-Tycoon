Idle Roundsman Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026. Applies to the Idle Roundsman app for Android and the web version of the game.

In short: Idle Roundsman has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts or location. Your game is saved only on your own device. In the Android app, ads play only when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward. Those ads come from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. Purchases go through Google Play. We never sell personal information.

What the app stores

The game saves your progress (your company name, money, trucks, towns, trophies and settings) in the app's own storage on your device. We never receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or press Company > Settings > Erase.

If your phone's own backup service is turned on (for example Google Backup on Android), your phone may include the game's save in your personal device backup. That backup is controlled by you and your phone's maker, and we cannot see it.

Optional reward ads (Android app)

The game shows an ad only when you tap a button such as Watch ad to get a reward, like double income for 30 minutes. There are no banner ads and no ads that play on their own.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, count them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize them, Google collects information from your device: the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. This is handled under Google's privacy policy and Google's explanation of how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad can load, and you can change your choice any time at Company > Settings > Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings under Privacy > Ads.

The Supporter Pack (below) gives every reward without an ad, and the app then stops loading ads.

Purchases (Android app)

The optional Supporter Pack is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the pack, so it can come back after a reinstall or on a new device. Google handles that information under Google's privacy policy.

The web version

The web version has no ads and no purchases. When opened in a browser it downloads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of any normal web request.

Save codes

The Copy button turns your save into a text code so you can move your game to another device. The code is created only when you press the button and goes only where you paste it.

Permissions

The Android app uses internet access and the advertising ID permission for ads, and the Google Play billing permission for purchases. It never asks for location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

Idle Roundsman is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third parties

The Android app contains Google AdMob (ads, with Google's consent tool) and Google Play Billing (purchases). It contains no analytics, crash reporting or social media code.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
