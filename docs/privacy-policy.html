Trash Tycoon Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026. Applies to the Trash Tycoon app for Android and iPhone and the web version of the game.

In short: Trash Tycoon does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Your game is saved only on your own device.

What the app stores

The game saves your progress (your company name, money, trucks, towns, trophies and settings) in the app's own storage on your device. This information never leaves your device through the app. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or press Company > Settings > Erase.

If your phone's own backup service is turned on (for example Google Backup on Android), your phone may include the game's save in your personal device backup. That backup is controlled by you and your phone's maker, and we cannot see it.

What the app sends over the internet

Nothing. The Android app does not request internet access. The game does not contact any server, including ours.

The web version of the game, when opened in a browser, downloads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of any normal web request. See Google's privacy policy.

Save codes

The Copy button turns your save into a text code so you can move your game to another device. The code is created only when you press the button and goes only where you paste it.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media code.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/MHelton1/Trash-Tycoon/issues.
